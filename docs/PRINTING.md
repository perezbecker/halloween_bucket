# CORE One+ Single-Nozzle Printing

## Material And Printer Setup

The [USB jobs and checklist](../usb/COREONE_04HF_PLA/START_HERE.txt) are actual
ASCII G-code generated by **PrusaSlicer 2.9.6**, using the official
**Prusa CORE One HF0.4 nozzle** machine profile and **0.20mm STRUCTURAL** print
profile from PrusaResearch bundle **2.5.10**. Its COREONE firmware profile is
used for the single-tool CORE One+. Machine startup, cleaning, probing, chamber
control, and shutdown scripts are retained, not replaced with generic G-code.

**Your actual machine/nozzle/filament were not confirmed.** Use these pre-sliced
files only on a stock single-tool CORE One+ with a **0.4 mm high-flow nozzle**,
current stable compatible firmware, **1.75 mm PLA**, and a **smooth PEI sheet**.
The emitted firmware notice is **6.8.1+16182**. Set the printer's actual nozzle
diameter, high-flow, and hardened flags correctly and do not bypass warnings.

The glow job requires a **wear-resistant/hardened nozzle**, and glow PLA rated
for that nozzle's 0.4 mm bore. Both materials must tolerate **220 C first layer,
215 C afterwards, and a 60 C bed**. If the filament manufacturer requires a
0.6 mm nozzle or different temperatures, reslice. **Glow PETG, including
Prusament PETG Ultraglow, must not use the supplied PLA G-code.**

The faceplate is mechanically attached, so other material combinations are
possible after appropriate reslicing and testing; unlike the earlier co-print,
they need not bond together. The supplied G-code remains PLA-only. A printer,
nozzle, sheet, material, or firmware mismatch is not resolved by ignoring its
warning. No MMU, INDX, second nozzle, or mid-print filament swap is needed.

## Print From USB

1. Check the prerequisites above and read the
  [USB checklist](../usb/COREONE_04HF_PLA/START_HERE.txt).
2. Put the five supplied `.gcode` files onto a FAT32 USB drive and safely eject
  it. Do not format a drive containing needed data. The printer runs the G-code,
  not STL/STEP/3MF source files or a zip archive.
3. Clear the build sheet, load the correct filament through the printer's
  Filament menu, insert the USB drive, and select the matching job in Print.
4. Check the model/nozzle/material notices and observe the automatic cleaning,
  levelling, and first layer. Stop if material fails to adhere or extrudes badly.
5. Let the sheet cool, remove the part and loose material, then load the next
  job. Change filament between jobs using the printer menu. Do not leave a
  finished part on the sheet for the next job.

Complete the first four parts and the physical fit test **before printing the
full cauldron**. You can do black jobs 01, 03, and 04 first to avoid extra swaps,
then glow job 02, test the assembly, and finally black job 05. No electronics,
glass, battery, or rope are installed in the printer during any job.

The cauldron's 3 mm nominal wall, 3.6 mm floor, and rolled rim/bosses are modeled geometry.
Do not scale them down or use spiral vase mode. Most thin walls print effectively
solid regardless of the nominal infill percentage.

The reusable [black profile](../profiles/core_one_plus_0.4HF_black_PLA.ini) and
[glow profile](../profiles/core_one_plus_0.4HF_glow_PLA.ini) contain the full
resolved settings. For another setup, load the geometry into current PrusaSlicer,
select the correct official printer/nozzle/material presets, retain adequate
wall thickness, and inspect the preview. Do not reuse this G-code after a nozzle
change without matching its 0.4 HF assumptions or reslicing.

4. [04_guard_black.3mf](../print/04_guard_black.3mf): the battery cradle and candy

5. [05_cauldron_black.3mf](../print/05_cauldron_black.3mf): the black cauldron,

Every job is **one material on extruder 1**. Model bounds and all emitted model
moves, including supports and brims, are checked against the CORE One+
**250 x 220 x 270 mm** volume. The 224 x 209 x 200 mm cauldron has an initial
5 mm XY placement allowance. Official startup cleaning/purging intentionally
uses Y = -2.5 before layer 1; that machine-specific path is retained unchanged.
Do not move the models closer to the bed edges or scale them to a larger size.

## Support And Preview Checks

The black eye bore, rope bosses, and portions of the rolled lip may need
localized supports. The separately printed flat skull does not. The lower bowl and
inward shoulder are shaped to stay above 55 degrees to the bed, avoiding broad
support structures under the belly or inside the neck. The reference audit uses automatic organic supports for
the bucket and coupon. Do not assume either is globally support-free.
Keep support and support-interface extruders on **the same single black extruder**.
Leave supports accessible for removal through the mouth
or eye opening. Never leave support remnants under a PCB post or against glass.

Before printing, inspect the sliced layers around:

- The entire glow faceplate must lie flat at Z = 0 and finish at Z = 3 mm.
  Its high location on the assembled cauldron is not its print orientation.
- The eye roof and ring: no unhandled bridging or support welded into the bore.
- All four M2 holes and the M2.5 carrier passages: they must remain open.
- The rope anchors and rim: continuous black perimeters, not disconnected shells.
- The carrier's small nut-pocket roofs and guard strap slots: short bridges
  should be achievable with the calibrated material; test first.

The black wall is now continuous and full thickness behind the skull, except
for the eye opening and screw holes. There is **no inlay cutout or material
bonding requirement**. The 3 mm skull contacts the outside wall and is retained
by four separate M2 x 10 screws, washers, and nuts.

## The 1 kg Limits

[CAD validation](validation.json) budgets **907.59 g black** and **105.71 g glow**:
all parts including the coupon, solid-volume density bounds, plus 125 g black
and 50 g glow allowances. The separate
[native slicing audit](slicer_validation.json) estimates **771.49 g black** and
**55.89 g glow** for all five supplied jobs, including supports, brims, and startup purge.

The black allowance is intentionally the controlling limit. Add the per-tool
usage from **all five plates**, including supports, priming, and any
machine-preset purge not included in the slicer's totals. Use the actual
filament density, make sure there is enough usable filament on the spool, and
do not start if either total reaches **1,000 g**. Extra coupons/failed prints
consume the same spool budget and are not included.

These figures assume black/glow density at most **1.30/2.00 g/cm3** and one copy
of each part. Manual loading purges, retries, and extra coupons use the same
spool budget. Do not reduce wall perimeters or rope-anchor strength to save
filament. Supplied G-code is ready for the stated setup, not a guarantee that
an unconfirmed printer/material combination will print successfully.
