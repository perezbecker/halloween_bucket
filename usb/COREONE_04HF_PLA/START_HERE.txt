These are actual single-nozzle G-code jobs generated with PrusaSlicer 2.9.6

REQUIRED SETUP
  Printer: stock Prusa CORE One+, single tool, no MMU or INDX.
  Firmware: current stable firmware compatible with the official profile.
            The files carry the 6.8.1+16182 update notice.
  Nozzle: 0.4 mm HIGH-FLOW. Wear-resistant/hardened is REQUIRED for glow PLA.
          Set the printer's nozzle diameter, high-flow, and hardened flags
          correctly. Do not bypass a nozzle/model mismatch warning.
  Filament: 1.75 mm black PLA / glow-in-the-dark PLA.
            Both must support 220 C first layer, 215 C thereafter.
            Glow filament must be rated for a 0.4 mm hardened nozzle.
  Sheet: clean smooth PEI, suitable for PLA, at 60 C.
  Chamber: official automatic control, target 20 C, vents unobstructed.

NOT FOR a 0.6 mm nozzle, standard-flow nozzle, PETG, TPU, ASA, or other printer.
Prusament PETG Ultraglow is PETG: DO NOT use the glow PLA G-code for it.
If your setup differs, reslice the supplied 3MF with the matching official
printer and filament profiles. Never fix a mismatch by ignoring the warning.

USB WORKFLOW
1. Use a readable FAT32 USB drive; do not format a drive containing needed data.
2. Copy the five .gcode files from this folder onto the drive, then safely eject.
   The printer runs .gcode, not .stl, .step, .3mf, or a zip archive.
3. Insert the drive in the CORE One+. Clear the sheet completely and load the
   filament stated in the filename through the printer's Filament menu.
4. Select the job in the printer's Print menu. Check the model/nozzle notices,
   expected material, and available filament before starting. Observe the
   cleaning, levelling, and first layer. Stop if it lifts or extrudes poorly.
5. Let the sheet cool and remove each finished part before starting the next job.
   Change colors only BETWEEN jobs through the printer menu.

SCULPTED SKULL
  Up to 10.5 mm total thickness, with the original flat back and 3 mm screw seats.
  Forehead, brow, cheeks, jaw and teeth grow outward only. Eye clearance unchanged.
  Print flat back down, sculpture up; 0.10 mm layers (0.20 first), 100% infill.
  No supports or brim. Reuse the same M2 x 10 screws, washers and nuts.

ASSEMBLY SUMMARY
  Skull to wall: four M2 x 10 screws, four M2 washers, four M2 nuts.
  Carrier to wall: four M2 x 35 screws, washers and nuts (separate holes).
  Stock lens hardware remains M2.5; do not substitute M2 in M2.5 threads.
  The lens is retained by Adafruit's acrylic kit, not by the skull plate.
  Use the complete hardware list and fit-test steps in docs/ASSEMBLY.md
  in the repository before assembling powered electronics.

The full skull and battery guard extend below the coupon's foot. Hold the
coupon above a padded surface/bench edge during the assembled fit test.
Never install electronics or the LiPo during printing. Never crush the pouch.
Use wrapped candy/a liner. Check hanging balance and rope lugs over a soft
surface before use. The rope holes stay fixed at their existing coordinates;
the heavier sculpted plate predicts about 2.1 degrees of empty forward tilt.
Uneven candy, actual hardware weights, and rope knots will change it.
