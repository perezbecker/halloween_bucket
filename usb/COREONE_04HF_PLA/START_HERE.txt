CYCLOPS CAULDRON - PRUSA CORE ONE+
PRINTING CHECKLIST

This folder contains ready-to-print G-code for the bucket, skull, electronics
carrier, battery/candy guard and a small mounting-test wall.
The design has been printed and reported to assemble and work successfully.
Confirm your own printer, material and hardware fit before use.

1. CHECK THE PRINTER AND MATERIAL

  Printer: Prusa CORE One+, single tool, no MMU or INDX.
  Nozzle: 0.4 mm HIGH-FLOW. Glow PLA also requires a wear-resistant/hardened
          nozzle and filament rated for its 0.4 mm bore.
  Filament: 1.75 mm PLA, rated for 220 C first layer and 215 C afterwards.
  Sheet: clean smooth PEI suitable for PLA, at 60 C.
  Firmware: compatible with the official COREONE profile.
            The files include the 6.8.1+16182 firmware notice.
  Chamber: official automatic control, nominal 20 C; keep vents unobstructed.

  Do not bypass printer/nozzle warnings. These files are NOT for a standard-flow
  nozzle, a different nozzle diameter, another printer, or PETG/TPU/ASA.
  Glow PETG is NOT glow PLA. Reslice the supplied 3MFs if your setup differs.

2. CHOOSE THE JOBS

  Print ONE skull: WHITE or GLOW.
  Print ONE carrier and ONE guard: use combined 03+04 OR separate 03 and 04.
  Do not print both alternatives unless you want extra copies.

  Filename                                  Filament        Estimate     Time
  01_test_BLACK_04HF_PLA.gcode               PLA             37.27 g     1 h 47 m
  02_skull_WHITE_04HF_PLA.gcode              white PLA       64.17 g     4 h 52 m
  02_skull_GLOW_04HF_PLA.gcode               glow PLA        98.72 g     5 h 40 m
  03_04_carrier_guard_BLACK_04HF_PLA.gcode   PLA            109.53 g     5 h 17 m
  03_carrier_BLACK_04HF_PLA.gcode            PLA             26.20 g     1 h 32 m
  04_guard_BLACK_04HF_PLA.gcode              PLA             83.38 g     3 h 44 m
  05_cauldron_BLACK_04HF_PLA.gcode           PLA            622.99 g    26 h 22 m

  BLACK and WHITE describe the intended colours; conventional PLA in another
  colour is usable when it meets the same temperature requirements.
  The GLOW job has different flow and abrasive-filament settings.
  All jobs use one filament and one extruder, without mid-print colour changes.

  The combined job prints both parts layer-by-layer, NOT sequential objects.
  Both parts keep their back-down orientations, with 15 mm between them.
  Have about 130 g of PLA available for this plate, allowing margin.

3. PRINT THE SMALL PARTS AND CHECK FIT

  Print the test wall, chosen skull, carrier and guard BEFORE the full bucket.
  Follow docs/ASSEMBLY.md in the repository to check hardware, lens alignment,
  battery fit and guard clearance on the test wall.
  Hold the test assembly above the bench: the skull and guard extend below
  the coupon's foot. Reuse all tested parts and hardware in the full bucket.

4. RUN A JOB FROM USB

  Copy the selected .gcode files to a readable FAT32 USB drive and safely eject.
  Do not format a drive containing needed data. The printer runs .gcode,
  not .stl, .step, .3mf, or a zip archive.
  Clear the entire sheet, load PLA through the printer's filament menu,
  insert the drive and select the job.
  Check the notices and watch cleaning, levelling and the first layer.
  On the combined plate, inspect the first layer of BOTH parts.
  Stop if material lifts or extrudes poorly.
  Let the sheet cool and remove each print before starting another job.
  Change filament BETWEEN jobs. Never install electronics or a battery during
  printing.

5. PRINT THE CAULDRON AND ASSEMBLE

  After the small assembly passes its fit checks, print job 05 and transfer
  the skull, carrier, lens/PCB module and guard to the full bucket.

  Skull to wall: four M2 x 10 screws, M2 washers and nuts.
  Carrier to wall: four M2 x 20 screws, M2 washers and nuts.
    Seat these nuts at the BOTTOM of the 15.9 mm deep rear rectangular wells.
  Guard to carrier: four M2 x 20 screws, M2 washers and nuts.
  PCB/lens module: four M2.5 x 20 screws with 1 mm insulating washers.
    Keep the stock acrylic, four 6 mm M2.5 standoffs and front M2.5 x 5 screws.
    M2 and M2.5 are different threads; do not substitute one for the other.
  Full hardware quantities and installation details are in docs/ASSEMBLY.md.

FILAMENT PLANNING

  With combined carrier/guard: 769.79 g for bucket, test wall, carrier and guard.
  With separate carrier/guard: 769.84 g for those parts.
  Add ONE skull: 64.17 g white PLA OR 98.72 g glow PLA.
  Estimates include automatic startup purge, using densities of 1.30 g/cm3
  conventional PLA and 2.00 g/cm3 glow. Leave extra for manual loading,
  retries and spares; these are not measured print weights.

  Skull jobs: 0.10 mm layers, 0.20 mm first layer, 100% rectilinear infill.
  Other jobs: 0.20 mm layers and nominal 15% gyroid infill.
  All use seven perimeters. The combined carrier/guard paths fill the printed
  sections with perimeters and solid layers; no sparse infill is emitted.

BEFORE CARRYING OR SERVICING

  Check rope knots, balance and anchors just above a padded surface, empty
  first and then with the intended load. There is no certified load rating.
  Do not swing the bucket or use the rope as a neck strap.
  Use wrapped candy or a suitable liner. Protect the glass from impacts.
  Keep electronics dry. Do not crush the LiPo pouch or overtighten its strap.
  Charge under supervision in an open nonflammable location, with the bucket
  empty and guard removed; never charge under candy or use a damaged battery.
